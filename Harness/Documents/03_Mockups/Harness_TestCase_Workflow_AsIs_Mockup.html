Reading the diagram as a whole The happy path — prompt in, cases out, run, pass — works. Every branch off it either loses information or loses the work entirely. Generation failure disguises itself as a review request 2; duplicates are detected but not shown when they could be acted on 5; failures are recorded without the one fact that would explain them 7; and the triage step that should close the loop is skipped in 86 of 87 cases 8.

The cheapest high-value fixes are all in the branches, not the path: make 2 impossible by deriving status from what was produced, populate the failing step for 7, and find out why 8 never happens. None of the three requires a new screen.
