### Detailed requirements (v1.4)

#### A. Preconditions and dependencies

* Enumeration drift check vs **[KS-991](https://gendvn.atlassian.net/browse/KS-991)** before claiming pass/fail.
* Vendor-approved **read-only** query or filter for first live run; **no** destructive SQL.

#### B. When `read_data` is absent (default through May 2026)

* Publish matrix cells **S** with rationale; keep **High-risk tool checklist** row open with status **Planned**.
* **Do not** substitute `list_table` / `describe_table` flows—these are **not** v1.4 tools.

#### C. When `read_data` is present

* **Prompt (example):** *Using `read_data`, return a small authorized sample of fund-related rows (e.g. first 10 rows per vendor-approved filter).*
* Validate structured columns, **row cap**, truncation flags, and **authorization** (black-box—no Dynamo UI).
* **Errors:** malformed/out-of-scope queries must fail with explicit errors—no broad dumps.

#### D. Matrix

* Guide **section 6** row **5.5 Tabular read (`read_data`)** — coordinate with **[KS-993](https://gendvn.atlassian.net/browse/KS-993)** (all non–n/a cells **S** until live, or **P**/**F** once executed).

### Acceptance criteria (BDD, v1.4)

_Scenario 1 — Happy path_

* **Given** `read_data` is **registered** and an authorized sample query exists
* **When** the tester runs section **5.5**
* **Then** returned rows are structured, capped, and in-scope; repeat-call stability documented

_Scenario 2 — Error path_

* **Given** invalid or out-of-scope query
* **When** invoked
* **Then** schema/API error—no unrelated table dump

_Scenario 3 — Edge case (default today)_

* **Given** `read_data` is **not** registered
* **When** the ticket is executed for v1.4
* **Then** outcomes are **S** with written rationale and checklist row remains **Planned/tracked**
